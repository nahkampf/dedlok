## Cyberspace Corps

### Kurogane Systems
Massproduced consumer electronics. Makes cheap, low-end cyberdecks and a few entry-level augmentations.

### Veritas
A company that primarily produces electronics for the corporate world. Office-style decks, small company mainframes, productivity software etc.

### Aegis
An upcoming cyberspace electronics manufacturer from the Netherlands, specializing in reliable, cost-effective electronics paired with minimalist design.

### Paladin
American cyberspace manufacturer with some state and military contracts. Produces expensive but reliable cyberspace gear with a focus on performance rather than power efficiency.

### Tsukuyomi-Wervel
Japanese-German manufacturer of high-end cyberspace hardware. Has a sideline in the infrastructure sector (fiber optics, satellite links, networking infrastructure etc). Produces premium cyberspace gear, with a brutalist design feel.

### Hosaka
The top-of-the-line producer of specialized cyberdecks, often customized and highly modular. Some of the most expensive tech available to civilians. Uses japanese names.

### Dunkelwerk
A secretive hacktivist maker collective that has been producing some really sleek prototype decks. These are highly experimental, and powerful (when they don't overheat and let the magic smoke out), and getting your hands on one of their toys is almost impossible.

### THO
The Tailored Hardware Operations office of the Special Operations Command is where the military cooks up their most specialized electronic and cyber warfare tools. Getting your hands on a THO deck without a very specific security clearance would be nigh impossible, not to mention very detrimental to your legal standing.
